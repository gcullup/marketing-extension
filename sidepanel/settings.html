Daily Scan Limits

Total profiles evaluated per day, regardless of outcome (rejected, needs review, or auto-approved) — not how many friend requests get sent. Set much higher than your send limits below, since most scanned profiles won't become requests. Set a day to 0 to skip scanning that day.

Daily Send Limits

Facebook's own soft pending-request ceiling is ~200. Variable per day of week, same as Daily Scan Limits above — this is what the Send Queue actually enforces, independent of how many were scanned or queued that day.

Messaging is the more heavily policed surface — keep this conservative. Enforced by the DM Queue via countDmSentToday.

Timing

Pause between finishing one candidate and starting the next during a Discovery Batch run — only applied after real Facebook interaction, not after an instant already-known skip. Revised 2026-09-01: the batch now schedules its next step via chrome.alarms (so it survives the background service worker being killed — see ARCHITECTURE.md hazard #2), and Chrome will not fire an alarm sooner than ~30 seconds out no matter what's requested. In practice this means the real pause is max(30s, your setting) — values below 30s (including the defaults) are silently floored to ~30s by Chrome itself, not by this extension.

Request Cleanup

A friend request that's still pending after this many days gets cancelled during "Check Accepted Friends," instead of sitting forever.
